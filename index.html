ScrapeAtlas

About

ScrapeAtlas makes it easy for developers and businesses to access public data from 38 platforms through one simple API. From Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube to Reddit, X, and LinkedIn, you can retrieve profiles, posts, videos, comments, transcripts, ads, products, and reviews as structured JSON.

Whether you're monitoring brands, discovering creators, researching competitors, or building AI agents with social context, ScrapeAtlas helps you get the data you need. Learn more and explore the platform at ScrapeAtlas.

ScrapeAtlas supports HTTP integration, a convenient CLI, and MCP for AI agents. You can also explore endpoints through the interactive playground, start with 100 free credits, and use pay-as-you-go credit packs with no subscription and no expiration.
